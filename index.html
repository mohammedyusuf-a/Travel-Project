1. Transparent Rupee (₹) Pricing

All tour packages, flight estimates, hotel stays, bus transfers, food allocations, and photographer fees are displayed in Indian Rupees (INR - ₹).

2. Dedicated Photographer Sessions

Photographers provided through Life Journey are certified professionals with high-resolution mirrorless / DSLR equipment and licensed drone operators where permitted by local law.

🔒 Privacy Policy & Data Protection

Effective Date: October 2026 · Life Journey

1. Information We Collect

Life Journey collects minimal personal data required to plan your travel, including name, email, saved itineraries, and wallet balance. We never sell your personal information or location coordinates.

2. UPI Financial Security

We do not store bank account passwords, UPI PINs, or debit card numbers on our servers. All UPI transactions adhere strictly to RBI and NPCI guidelines.
