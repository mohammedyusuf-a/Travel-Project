## 🌟 Overview

**Life Journey** is an all-in-one travel planning and management platform designed with complete financial transparency in Indian Rupees (₹). It provides day-by-day tour itineraries, exact expense breakdowns (flights, hotels, bus transfers, food & private photographer), an interactive ESRI Satellite World Map, and real-time UPI payment simulations (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM QR).

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 1. 🧭 Dedicated Tour Guide Engine
- **Enga Pora (Where We Go)**: Curated list of iconic landmarks, viewpoints, and hidden gems for each destination.
- **Enna Pandram (What We Do)**: Handpicked adventures, cultural rituals, boat cruises, and private photography sessions.
- **Enna Schedule (Hourly Itinerary)**: Day 1, Day 2, Day 3 morning-to-night timetables so travelers never miss a highlight.
- **Instant Destination Switching**: Quick chip carousel to jump between 30 global & domestic places.

### 2. 💰 Transparent Rupee (₹) Expense Breakdowns
Every destination features an itemized cost calculation:
- ✈️ **Flight Ticket**: Roundtrip airfare from India (DEL / BOM hubs).
- 🏨 **Room / Hotel Stay**: 4-Star boutique hotel, caldera cliff suite, or overwater bungalow.
- 🚌 **Bus / Coach**: AC Volvo or private chauffeured transfers.
- 🍽️ **Food & Dining**: Multi-cuisine breakfasts, authentic local delicacies, and fine dining.
- 📸 **Personal Photographer**: Professional DSLR photoshoot + drone 4K social reels.
- ✨ **Total All-Inclusive Tour Package**: Clear all-inclusive total with instant UPI checkout.

### 3. 🛰️ Satellite World View & Flight Trajectories
- Powered by high-resolution **ESRI World Imagery**.
- Realistic deep-space atmospheric orbit view with Earth curvature glow.
- Real-time curved flight path arcs drawn from India to destination airports.
- Map layer switcher: **Satellite World View**, **Street Map**, and **Dark Mode**.

### 4. 🌍 30 Real World & Domestic Destinations
- **International**: Santorini, Kyoto, Bali, Paris, Machu Picchu, Serengeti Safari, New York City, Amalfi Coast, Queenstown, Maldives, Iceland, Petra, Swiss Alps, Rome, Tokyo, Barcelona, Cairo, Banff National Park, Sydney, Cape Town, Rio de Janeiro, Dubai, Singapore, Cappadocia, London, Venice.
- **Incredible India**: Taj Mahal (Agra), Manali & Rohtang Pass, Ooty & Nilgiri Hills, Goa & Gokarna.

### 5. 🔒 Project Creator Admin Gate
- Administration portal is strictly restricted to the **Project Creator** (`creator2026`).
- Secure passcode verification modal prevents unauthorized access.
- KPI metrics tracking total UPI transaction volume, destination catalog, active bookings, and customer review moderation.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- **Frontend**: Semantic HTML5, Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+), Modern Vanilla CSS3 (Custom Design System, Glassmorphism & Orbit Animations)
- **Mapping**: Leaflet.js with ESRI World Imagery & CARTO Basemaps
- **Analytics & Charts**: Chart.js for Rupee budget breakdowns
- **Live Weather**: Open-Meteo REST API (real-time temperature & conditions)
- **Icons**: Lucide Icons & UTF-8 Travel Glyphs
